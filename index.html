Chapter One

The Truth I Want You To Know

Mary, we were once something real.

A lot happened. Some of it was painful. Some of it was confusing. And if I’m honest — a lot of it was my fault.

I’m not here to rewrite history or pretend I didn’t make mistakes. I know I hurt you. I know I made things heavier than they needed to be, and I know distance did not make it easier.

I know we live far apart, and sometimes the only chances we get to be close are rare and fragile.

But even with all of that…

You remain my favorite person in this world.

Even when we don’t talk every day. Even when life pulls us in different directions. Even when silence sits between us.

We spoke about making things right. I remember that. I’m not asking you to forget the past. I’m asking for a chance to show you that I can do this differently — with more honesty, more patience, and more care.
